# Privacy Policy (draft for legal review)

Last updated: 8 November 2026. This is a draft. It must be reviewed by a lawyer before publication (docs/legal-review.md L6).

ExamRadar ("we") is an independent app that shows Central and Kerala government exam dates collected from official websites. We are not affiliated with any government body.

## What we collect and why

| Data | When | Purpose | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|---|
| An anonymous device token | First launch | To keep your saved exams and send alerts. It is not linked to your name, phone number, or email. | Our server |
| Saved exams and your progress (for example "applied") | When you save an exam | To show your deadlines and send reminders | Your phone and our server |
| Push notification token | If push is enabled | To deliver alerts | Our server |
| Date of birth, category, community, qualifications, languages, disability and ex-serviceman status | Only if you add them in "My details" | To check whether you can apply for an exam | Your phone only, unless you turn on "Use my details for matching alerts" |
| Profile stored for matching | Only after you turn on matching alerts and agree | To find new exams you can apply for and alert you | Our server |
| Text or links you paste into Scam Check | When you use Scam Check | To check it against official sources | Our server, deleted within 24 hours. The result is kept for up to 90 days, without your text. |
| PDFs you upload to Scam Check | When you use Scam Check | To read and check the text | Processed in memory and not stored |

We do not collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos, Aadhaar number, portal passwords, or payment details. Photo and signature checks, and name and date-of-birth checks, run only on your phone.

## Legal basis

We process personal data only with your consent, given when you turn on a feature that needs it. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app. Your data is then deleted from our server.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. Service providers that host our server or deliver notifications (for example Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) process data only on our instructions.

## Your rights

In Settings you can:

- download all the data we hold about you (right to access);
- correct your details;
- delete all your data from the phone and our server (right to erasure).

You can also contact the grievance officer: [name, email and address to be added before release].

## Retention

- Account data is kept until you delete it, or for 12 months after the app was last used.
- Scam Check text is deleted within 24 hours.
- Server logs never contain profile details. They are kept for 30 days.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored on access-controlled servers. Admin access uses a password and a one-time code.

## Children

ExamRadar is intended for adults (18 and over).

## Changes

We will show changes to this policy in the app before they take effect.
